Course Objectives:
To understand the principles and architecture of distributed systems and cloud
computing.
To analyze the challenges in designing and implementing distributed systems.
To introduce cloud models, services, and deployment strategies.
To gain practical knowledge of cloud infrastructure and virtualization.
To familiarize students with emerging trends like edge computing, containers, and
serverless computing.
